Parametric forms. Drag a slider — the physics follows live.

Pole by pole

Choose Pole on the canvas (or press P). Click a start point, then move the mouse: up raises the pole, down brings it to the ground. Slide onto a coloured line to snap: X (red) and Y (green) are level, Z (blue) is up. Press ↑ to lock Z. Type a length and press Enter for an exact pole.

Hillside

Generators re-seat their stone plinths on the new ground automatically. Every structure stores its site position, ready for the multi-building site mode later.
